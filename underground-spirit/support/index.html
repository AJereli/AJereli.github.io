Questions, bugs and feedback

Write to anatoliy.valeev97@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Reporting a bug

It helps a lot if you include:

  • your device model and iOS or iPadOS version,
  • what you were doing when it happened,
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you can.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. The game works fully offline and has no accounts, so progress can't be moved between devices or restored after the app is deleted.

How do I start over?

Delete your save from the start screen, then begin a new game.
