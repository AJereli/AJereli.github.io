Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Underground Spirit does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game works fully offline, and everything it saves stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs, and it does not connect to the internet. We don't receive any information about you or how you play.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

You can delete a save at any time from the start screen. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These are collected by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and contain no personal information. We use them only to fix bugs.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
